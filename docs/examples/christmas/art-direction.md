# Recursos gráficos de Navidad

El marco y el fondo se generaron con la herramienta integrada ImageGen. Los PNG se incluyen sin edición posterior; los metadatos nine-slice determinan su escala lógica. Los sprites pequeños y la fuente tienen código fuente editable en `tools/GenerateChristmasAssets.java`.

## Prompt del marco

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: actual reusable nine-slice game GUI panel texture, NOT a mockup. Create an ORIGINAL Christmas pixel-art interface panel in the richly ornamented retro RPG style: carved chestnut wooden rectangular frame, gilded beveled inner lip, burgundy red velvet edge, deep very dark evergreen near-flat empty center for readable chat. Single perfectly front-facing square panel fills the square image. Crisp pixel-art hand-crafted clusters, no blur, no smooth vector look. Thick angular wooden frame occupies outer 12 percent each side, frame corner ornaments occupy outer 20 percent only. Elaborate holly sprigs, glossy red berries, tiny brass bells, red ribbons and snow piles ONLY at four corners; lower corners include a small wrapped gift and stone footing. The top center and bottom center are simple repeatable carved wood strips, side centers are simple repeatable wood strips. Central 65 percent is perfectly empty very dark green (#102923), no texture noise, no objects, no lettering, no controls. Leave transparent outer cutout areas around irregular corner holly and snow silhouette, while keeping the entire large center opaque dark green. Flat top-down asset suitable for nine-slice scaling; visually spectacular detailed pixel art at corners but clean regular edges. No text, no logos, no watermark, no inventory cells, no premade interface. This is a texture to draw behind functional Minecraft buttons and chat, not a screenshot. Use a 128x128 logical-pixel grid enlarged with hard nearest-neighbor blocks.

## Prompt del fondo

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: full-screen background texture for original Christmas pixel-art RPG GUI. Winter lodge courtyard at twilight, a warmly lit tiny timber cabin on the far right, fir trees heavily laden with snow on far left, muted snowy stone path along bottom, hanging distant brass lanterns, a few small snowflakes. Rich hand-crafted crisp retro pixel art similar to premium Minecraft fantasy GUI resource packs, nearest-neighbor hard block edges and restrained 128x72 logical-pixel composition enlarged. Wide landscape composition. Most of middle and upper center is calm dark navy blue night sky and dim forest; functional chat panels will cover middle so put detailed scenery near outer edges only. Palette midnight blue, desaturated pine, warm amber windows, snow ivory, sparse berry red details. No text, no lettering, no logos, no GUI panels, no border, no characters, no watermark. Actual flat background asset, NOT screenshot or mockup.
